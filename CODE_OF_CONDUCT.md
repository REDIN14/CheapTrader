# Code of conduct

CheapTrader is a small project made by people in their spare time. To keep it a place where people want to
help:

* Be kind and patient. Questions from beginners are welcome, and so are bug reports without a fix.
* Criticise the code, the idea or the behaviour of the program, never the person.
* No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere
  else the project is represented.
* Do not share other people's private information, and never post a MetaTrader login, password or account
  number (yours included).
* This project is not financial advice and nobody here is a financial adviser. Do not pressure anyone to trade,
  to copy a trade or to try a product.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who do not follow this. To report
a problem with someone's behaviour, write to the maintainers through the contact options on the profile of the
repository's owner, or use GitHub's *Report content* menu on the comment.

This text is short on purpose. It applies the same spirit as the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which you can read
for the long version.
