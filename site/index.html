TradingView-style charts for MetaTrader 5

CheapTrader is a Windows program that shows your MetaTrader 5 prices in a chart modelled on TradingView’s. Draw a long or short position on the chart and send it to your broker, write indicators in Python, or replay old prices bar by bar on a paper account.

Download for Windows All downloads and checksums

Installer, about 58 MB. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. MIT licence.

MetaTrader 5 keeps the connection to your broker. CheapTrader reads prices from it and sends orders through it. Windows will probably say it “protected your PC”, because the program is not code-signed: what to click.

What it does

Draw the trade, then send it

A long or short position box sits on the chart as it does in TradingView: entry, stop loss, target and the risk-to-reward ratio. In CheapTrader the box can also place the trade. Buy now or Sell now sends a market order with the box’s stop and target. Buy limit leaves a pending order at the box’s entry. The size comes from the distance to the stop: a percentage of your balance, a fixed amount of money, or a number of lots.

Orders
Market, limit and stop. Waiting orders are drawn on the chart and can be dragged.
Stop and target
Drag the tags on the chart or type the price. Both go to the broker with the order.
Drawings
Trend line, rectangle, parallel channel and the two position boxes. Saved per symbol; each one can be locked.

Indicators are Python functions

Six indicators are built in: SMA, EMA, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD and a range box, which draws the high-low range of the last 50 bars as an example of an indicator that draws shapes. For anything else you write a function that receives the candles as a pandas table and returns the lines to draw. It can draw on the price or in a pane of its own, and it can add shapes such as lines, boxes and labels.

def compute(df, params):
    n = int(params.get("period", 14))
    # df: time, open, high, low, close, tick_volume
    return {"Momentum": df["close"].diff(n)}
Sandbox
Imports are restricted. A run is stopped after 10 seconds or 2 GB of memory; both limits are settings in the .env file.
Editor
Write, save and switch on indicators in the Indicators panel. The documentation opens inside the program.

Replay old prices on a paper account

Click a bar and the replay starts there. Everything to the right of it stays hidden until the replay reaches it. Play, pause or step with the keyboard, at 0.5 to 50 bars per second. Buy and sell work as they do live, with stop loss and take profit. Every order fills at the close of the bar the replay is on, and stops and targets are checked bar by bar.

The paper account is a profile. Make as many as you like, each with a starting balance you choose. A profile keeps its balance and its trades when a replay ends and when you close the program. You can rename it, start it over or delete it from the menu next to the Replay tag.

Report
Equity curve, net profit, profit factor, win rate, maximum drawdown, average win and loss, and every trade with its symbol and how it ended.
Open positions
A position still open when a replay ends is closed at the last price and listed as “Replay ended”.

See how the account has done

The bar at the bottom of the chart shows the account’s return, its trades, win rate, largest drawdown and profit factor, worked out from your broker’s own history. Click the server’s name for the full report, the same one the replay has.

Trades
Every closed trade with its prices, how long it was held, what it cost in commission and swap, its result after those costs, and how it ended: stop loss, take profit, stop out or by hand.
Statistics
The figures of MetaTrader’s own report: expected payoff, recovery factor, Sharpe ratio, drawdowns, long and short trades, streaks, and what each instrument made.
Period
All time, this year, this month, this week or today. Deposits and withdrawals are left out of the curve and the percentages.

It finds MetaTrader for you

The first time you start it, a short tour checks four things: whether MetaTrader 5 is installed, whether it is running, whether an account is logged in, and whether CheapTrader is connected. It connects without a restart as soon as the answers are yes. There is no login number, password or file path to type.

Without MetaTrader the program still starts, on a synthetic market, so you can try the tools first.

Smaller things

  • Candles are built from every tick, with the terminal’s own highs and lows.
  • A watchlist of every symbol your broker offers.
  • Intervals from 1 minute to 1 month: 1, 5, 15 and 30 minutes, 1 and 4 hours, day, week, month.
  • History is fetched once and kept in a local database, so reopening a chart does not download it again.
  • The layout, the symbol, the interval and your drawings are remembered.
  • The MetaTrader window can be hidden from the program’s menu. A hidden terminal closes when the program does.
  • The program tells you when a newer release is on GitHub and can install it for you.

Install

  1. Install MetaTrader 5 from your broker and log in to a trading account. A demo account works. Leave it open; a minimised window is fine.
  2. Download the installer from the latest release and run it. It installs for the current user and needs no administrator rights. If you prefer, take the portable zip, unzip it anywhere and run CheapTrader.exe.
  3. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”, because the program is not code-signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway. To check that the file is the one that was published, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page: 
    Get-FileHash .\CheapTrader-<version>-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256
  4. The welcome tour opens by itself and connects to MetaTrader.

Updating

The program looks at GitHub’s release list when it starts and every six hours after that. When a newer release exists, an Update button appears at the top of the window. It shows what changed. If you click Install, the program downloads the installer, checks it against SHA256SUMS.txt, closes, runs the installer and opens again. Your data folder is not touched: drawings, indicators, settings, profiles and stored history stay where they are. A portable copy cannot install itself, so its update window links to the release page instead. You can switch the check off in the About window.

Where your data is

In a folder called data next to the program, or in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CheapTrader\data when the program’s own folder is not writable. Uninstalling leaves that folder alone. The program writes its log to data\logs.

What it does on your PC

Orders from the program are off in a fresh install. You switch them on in the MetaTrader menu at the bottom of the window. MetaTrader’s own Algo Trading button has to be green as well.

The program listens on 127.0.0.1 only. It checks the Host and Origin of every request, so a web page you have open cannot place an order through it.

There is no account and no telemetry. The only connection to the internet is the update check against GitHub, which you can switch off.

Python indicators run in a separate process with the time and memory limits above. To report a vulnerability, see SECURITY.md.

What it does not do

  • It runs on Windows 10 and 11 only. The MetaTrader 5 package it uses to reach the terminal exists only for Windows.
  • It works with MetaTrader 5, not MetaTrader 4 or any other platform.
  • It does not trade by itself. There are no strategies, no automation and no Expert Advisor.
  • It is not code-signed, so Windows asks you to confirm the first start.
  • In a replay every order fills at the bar’s close, and limit and stop orders are not available.
  • Indicators are written in Python, not in MQL5.
  • There is no phone or browser version.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT licence, with no paid version and no ads. If it is useful to you, bug reports and pull requests help most. You can also support it on Ko-fi.

Do I need MetaTrader 5?

For real prices and trading, yes. CheapTrader reads from your own MetaTrader 5 terminal, from any broker that offers MT5. A free demo account is enough.

Can it place orders without me?

No. It only sends what you click, and only after you have switched orders on. Trading carries a high risk of losing money, so try everything on a demo account first.

Is it affiliated with TradingView or MetaQuotes?

No. CheapTrader is independent. Its look is modelled on TradingView’s, and its charts are drawn by TradingView’s open-source Lightweight Charts library. MetaTrader 5 is a trademark of MetaQuotes Ltd.

Something does not work. Where do I say so?

Open an issue on GitHub and attach the last lines of data\logs.
